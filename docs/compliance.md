# Record checks and their sources

`npm run hotel -- compliance` (the `/compliance` command) checks the records against the rules below. Each check is a query in the `v_compliance` view in `supabase/migrations/0001_small_hotel.sql`, and the GST worked out on each stay is in the `v_night_gst` and `v_booking_gst` views. A clean result means the records are complete and consistent. It does not mean your tax is right: that is your accountant's call, and your duty.

Sources were read on 30 September 2026. Rules change. If a source says something different from this page, the source wins: update this page and the check together (`/customise`).

| Rule | What the data must show | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `NZ-LONG-STAY-GST` | Accommodation in a commercial dwelling (hotel, motel, B&B, serviced apartment) carries GST at 15%. After four weeks, GST is charged on 60% of the value of the domestic goods and services, and from the start of the stay when a stay of more than four weeks is agreed up front. The reduced value does not apply when the accommodation is supplied through an online marketplace. The system applies 60% from night 29 (or night 1 when `long stay agreed` is set on the booking) and skips bookings on channels of kind `ota`. The check lists every live stay over 28 nights so you can confirm the treatment. Combined charges (bed and breakfast) have to be split: post breakfast as its own charge. | [Inland Revenue: GST on short-stay and commercial accommodation](https://www.ird.govt.nz/property/commercial-property/gst-and-commercial-accommodation) |
| `AU-LONG-STAY-GST` | For long-term accommodation in commercial residential premises (28 or more continuous days, supplied to an individual), a provider can choose to charge GST on 50% of the GST-inclusive price, from the 28th day for premises that are not predominantly long-term. The system applies it from night 28 only when `au_long_stay_concession` is on in settings; otherwise the check lists each long stay charged full GST so you can decide with your accountant. Premises that are predominantly long-term (70% or more of guests staying 28 days or more) apply the concession from the first day: change the rule with `/customise` if that is you. | [ATO: Commercial residential property](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/in-detail/your-industry/property/gst-and-commercial-property/commercial-residential-property), [GSTR 2012/7](https://www.ato.gov.au/law/view/document?docid=GST/GSTR20127/NAT/ATO/00001) |
| `GST-NUMBER` | Invoices are being issued and settings has a GST number (NZ) or ABN (AU) to print on them. In NZ, taxable supply information for supplies over $200 shows the seller's GST number. | [Inland Revenue: How taxable supply information works](https://www.ird.govt.nz/gst/tax-invoices-for-gst/how-tax-invoices-for-gst-work), [ATO: Tax invoices](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/tax-invoices) |
| `NZ-INVOICE-OVER-1000` | Every invoice over NZ$1,000 has, on the guest record, the buyer's name and at least one identifier: address, phone, email or business number. Inland Revenue requires these for a GST-registered buyer on supplies over $1,000; the check asks for them on every such invoice because the front desk rarely knows who is registered. | [Inland Revenue: How taxable supply information works](https://www.ird.govt.nz/gst/tax-invoices-for-gst/how-tax-invoices-for-gst-work) |
| `AU-INVOICE-1000-PLUS` | Every invoice of A$1,000 or more shows the buyer's identity or ABN. | [ATO: Tax invoices](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/tax-invoices) |
| `PRIVACY-RETENTION` | No guest record whose last stay ended more than `privacy_review_years` ago (default 7) is kept without a decision. NZ Privacy Principle 9: an agency that holds personal information must not keep it for longer than is required for the purposes for which it may lawfully be used. Australian Privacy Principle 11.2 asks the same of APP entities. Tax records have their own keeping periods (7 years in NZ, 5 in AU): keep the invoice, anonymise the guest. | [Privacy Commissioner: Principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/), [OAIC: Australian Privacy Principles](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-quick-reference) |
| `CARD-DATA` | No guest note holds something shaped like a card number. Card numbers belong in your payment provider, not in free text. | Operator policy, with Privacy Principle 5 (storage and security) at the [Privacy Commissioner](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/) |
| `DOUBLE-BOOKING` | No room is sold twice for the same night, across your own bookings and the outside calendars you import. | Operator policy |

## What the checks do not do

- They do not file anything with Inland Revenue or the ATO, or know whether a guest is GST-registered.
- They do not cover liquor licensing, food safety, fire evacuation schemes, building warrants of fitness, pool safety or local short-stay rules. The evacuation roll (`npm run docs -- fire-roll`) is a list of who is in the building, not an evacuation scheme. Add a rule with `/customise` when your business needs one, with its source.
- They read your records. A booking taken on paper and never entered is invisible to them.

Nothing here is tax or legal advice.
